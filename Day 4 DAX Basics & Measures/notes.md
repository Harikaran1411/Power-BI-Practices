\## DAX Measures Practiced



```DAX

Total Sales = SUM(Orders\[Sales])



Total Profit = SUM(Orders\[Profit])



Total Orders = COUNTROWS(Orders)



Average Sales = AVERAGE(Orders\[Sales])



Maximum Sales = MAX(Orders\[Sales])



Minimum Sales = MIN(Orders\[Sales])

```

\## What I Learned



DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) is used in Power BI to create calculations and measures for data analysis.



I practiced creating basic measures using aggregation functions such as SUM, COUNTROWS, AVERAGE, MIN, and MAX.



I also learned that measures are calculated dynamically based on the filter and visual context, making them useful for creating interactive KPIs and business reports.
